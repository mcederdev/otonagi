Otonagi

The sound of a still sea

音凪

Coming soon to the App Store

Open Otonagi and music is already playing. Soft koto, shakuhachi and singing bowls that never play the same way twice, composed for you as they play, with rain, waves or a quiet fire beneath.

Moods

A place for every hour

Each mood has its own instruments, pace and light. Swipe to drift from one to the next. Tap a card to turn it over.

Ten moods in all. Nagi and Ame are free.

Inside

Quiet by design

音

Music that never repeats

Japanese instruments arranged live on your iPhone, in koto, shakuhachi, piano, bowls, pads or handpan.

雨

Nature beneath

Mix rain, waves, wind, birds, stream and fire under the music, or let the time of day choose.

息

Breathe

A short breathing exercise, guided by gentle haptics and a soft heartbeat. Saves mindful minutes to Apple Health if you like.

言

Today's word

One of 80 Japanese words to sit with each day. It waits for you after a gentle "How are you today?"

動

Move with the music

Ugoki (動き) in Kumo guides tai chi as it is practised in Japan. Hogushi (ほぐし) in Nobi guides gentle stretches. Himukae (日迎え) in Hinode is a sun salutation in twelve kata.

心

How do you feel now?

A short, kind check-in after Breathe, a movement session or a long listen, with a line on why you might feel that way.

波

Touch the water

Tap the screen and a ripple spreads, with a single note that fits the music. Its volume has its own slider.

守

Nothing leaves your phone

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your name and check-ins stay on your device.

眠

Sleep and Siri

A sleep timer that fades the music out, shown on the Lock Screen, and "Sleep with Otonagi" from Siri.
