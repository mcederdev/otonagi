Otonagi

Sound, and a calm sea.

Otonagi is a quiet place on your iPhone. Open it and music is already playing: soft koto, shakuhachi and bells that never repeat the same way twice, with rain, waves or a crackling fire beneath.

  • MoodsNagi, Ame, Tera, Mori, Umi, Yuki and Takibi. Each has its own instruments, pace and colour.
  • Nature soundsMix rain, waves, forest, snow and fire under the music.
  • BreatheA short breathing exercise guided by gentle haptics.
  • Today's wordOne Japanese word each day to sit with, and an optional "How are you today?" check-in.
  • Nothing leaves your phoneNo account, no ads, no tracking. Your name and check-ins stay on your device.

Otonagi is free to try, with Nagi and Ame included. Otonagi Plus opens every mood, style and sound for $24.99 a year or $49.99 once.
