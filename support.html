Common questions

I can't hear anything

Check the volume and that your headphones or speaker are connected. Tap the mood name at the top to make sure the music isn't paused. If it still stays quiet, close Otonagi fully (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again.

How do I pause the music?

Tap the name of the mood. Tap it again to carry on.

I bought Otonagi Plus but it isn't unlocked

Open Otonagi Plus from the menu in Otonagi and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone, choose Otonagi and tap Cancel Subscription. Plus stays unlocked until the end of the year you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Otonagi work offline?

Yes. All music and sounds are made on your iPhone, so no connection is needed.

Where is my check-in history kept?

Only on your iPhone. We never see it. Deleting the app erases it, along with your name and settings. See the Privacy Policy for more.

Is Otonagi on iPad or Android?

Otonagi is made for iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
