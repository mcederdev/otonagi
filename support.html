Common questions

I can't hear anything

Check the volume and that your headphones or speaker are connected. Tap the mood name at the top to make sure the music isn't paused. Music pauses when headphones are unplugged or a call comes in, and after a call it only comes back if it was playing before. If it still stays quiet, close Otonagi fully (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again.

How do I pause the music?

Tap the name of the mood. Tap it again to carry on.

I bought Otonagi Plus but it isn't unlocked

Open the menu in Otonagi, then Otonagi Plus, and tap Restore purchases. It is also on the Otonagi Plus screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In Otonagi, open the menu, then Otonagi Plus › Manage subscription. Or open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone, choose Otonagi and tap Cancel Subscription. Plus stays unlocked until the end of the month or year you paid for. To avoid being charged after the free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How does the free trial work?

The monthly plan starts with 7 days free. If you allow notifications, Otonagi reminds you before the trial ends. If you don't cancel, it becomes $4.99 a month. Yearly ($24.99) and lifetime ($49.99) have no trial.

What does Otonagi save in Apple Health?

Only three things, and only if you turn them on: mindful minutes from Breathe, Flexibility workouts from Hogushi in Nobi, and Yoga workouts from Himukae in Hinode. Each is off until you turn it on with its own switch in its sheet, and only sessions of a minute or more are saved. Otonagi never reads your health data. You can turn it off in the Health app under Sharing › Apps › Otonagi.

Why did Otonagi ask how I feel after listening?

After a session of 30 minutes or more, Otonagi can send one gentle "How do you feel now?" about 15 minutes after the music stops, if you allowed notifications. It never comes after the sleep timer, never between 22:00 and 08:00, and at most once a day. Turn it off in the menu under Check-ins. Your answers stay on your phone.

Where is today's word?

The word waits until you've done the day's check-in, or tap Just show me the word. It stays the same all day and changes at 04:00. There are 80 words; 27 are free and the rest come with Otonagi Plus.

What is Kumo?

Kumo (雲, cloud) is a Plus mood built around tai chi as it is practised in Japan: a still picture of a tai chi master in silhouette by the water, with a torii and a small hall on the far shore. In Kumo, the Breathe button becomes Ugoki (動き, movement). Tap it for a guided session of 6, 12 or 20 minutes: eight slow movements, one breath each, led by flute, pluck and bowl sounds, ending with "How do you feel now?". Move only within what is comfortable, and stop if anything hurts.

What is Nobi?

Nobi (伸, stretch) is a Plus mood: a man sitting in agura in a sunlit dojo, with handpan music in the Akebono scale. In Nobi, the Breathe button becomes Hogushi (ほぐし, loosening up), a gentle stretching guide with 30-second holds and a timer that counts up. When you finish, it can save a Flexibility workout to Apple Health if you turned that on, and then asks "How do you feel now?". Stretch only as far as feels comfortable, never into pain.

What is Hinode?

Hinode (日の出, sunrise) is a Plus mood: a figure on a hillside deck at dawn, with a cherry tree in bloom, Fuji in the haze and a stone lantern, to koto, the shō and birdsong. In Hinode, the Breathe button becomes Himukae (日迎え, welcoming the sun), a sun salutation (Surya Namaskar) in twelve kata with Japanese names. Its sheet shows each kata with why and how to do it and how to breathe into it. Each kata is held for 30 seconds, with 7 seconds to move into the next. A round takes about 7 minutes, the sun rises as you go, and it can be saved to Apple Health as a Yoga workout. Move only within what is comfortable.

Can I use Siri?

Yes. Try "Play Otonagi", "Play Ame in Otonagi", "Sleep with Otonagi", "Breathe with Otonagi" or "Pause Otonagi". You can also add these to the Shortcuts app.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Otonagi work offline?

Yes. All music and sounds are made on your iPhone, so no connection is needed.

Where is my check-in history kept?

Only on your iPhone. We never see it, and it is not shared with Apple Health. Deleting the app erases it, along with your name and settings. See the Privacy Policy for more.

Is Otonagi on iPad or Android?

Otonagi is made for iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
